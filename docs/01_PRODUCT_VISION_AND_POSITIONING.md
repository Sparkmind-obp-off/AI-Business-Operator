# 01 — Product Vision & Positioning

## Vision

Build an AI Business Operator that helps a human move from *"What should I build or sell?"* to *"Here is a real opportunity, here is the evidence, and here are the next actions"* with minimal manual work.

## Problem

Builders frequently spend time creating products before validating whether a meaningful market need exists. Valuable demand signals are fragmented across search, social communities, marketplaces, job boards, forums, and other public or authorized sources.

## Product promise

The system continuously turns scattered demand signals into:

- normalized demand records;
- evidence-backed opportunities;
- opportunity scores;
- recommended actions;
- executable tasks;
- feedback that improves future prioritization.

## Primary user

A solo builder, small agency, freelancer, or digital entrepreneur who wants to discover paid work and product opportunities faster.

## North-star outcome

Reduce the time between a real demand signal and a qualified, actionable business opportunity.

## Non-goals

- Do not manufacture demand.
- Do not claim that a lead will buy.
- Do not bypass authentication, access controls, paywalls, or platform restrictions.
- Do not make unofficial scraping the core architectural dependency.
- Do not autonomously send high-impact outreach without explicit user controls.

## Product loop

`Discover → Verify → Normalize → Score → Recommend → Execute → Measure → Learn`
